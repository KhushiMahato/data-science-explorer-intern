# Week 1 — Data Science Project Planning & Strategy Design

## Objective
Develop a comprehensive project plan and strategy for a data science project (Climate Risk & Business Impact Analyzer) — covering background, objectives, scope, methodology, timeline, tools, and expected outcomes.

## Deliverable
📄 [`Week1_Project_Plan.docx`](./Week1_Project_Plan.docx) — full project plan document

## Project Summary
The plan designs a **Climate Risk & Business Impact Analyzer**: a Python (and R)-driven pipeline and dashboard that quantifies climate and air-quality risk across 12 manufacturing facilities in India, using:
- **NASA POWER** (climate data)
- **OpenAQ API** (air quality)
- **World Bank CCKP** (climate projections)

## What's Inside the Report
1. Introduction & Background
2. Project Objectives & Scope
3. Methodology & Strategy (with flowchart + architecture diagram)
4. Timeline & Tools (~34 hours planned, with Gantt-style chart)
5. Expected Outcomes & Challenges (with risk/mitigation table)
6. Conclusion
